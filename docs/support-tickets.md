# Support tickets

An export from the support queue, covering roughly the last three months.

This is what players and support agents wrote, not what anyone has confirmed.
Agents do not have access to the games, so most of these are one person's
account of what they saw. Nothing here has been reproduced by engineering.

---

### PS-291 · Iron Siege · 14 Jan · open

Player bought the Siege Pass, the store showed them an error, and they wrote in
asking for their money back. By the time the agent looked, the pass was on the
account. The player had bought it a second time in the meantime.

### PS-304 · Nova Arena · 2 Feb · closed, no fault

"My gems never arrived." The agent found the purchase on a second account
belonging to the same player. No action taken.

### PS-318 · Iron Siege · 19 Feb · open

"The shop is empty." Player saw nothing available to buy for about ten minutes,
then it came back on its own. A different player reported the same thing the
same week.

### PS-330 · Tide Farm · 3 Mar · open

Player bought the Harvest Pass and says it never turned up in the game. They are
level 4. The agent could not see anything wrong with the order itself.

### PS-341 · Iron Siege · 11 Mar · open

"Took forever." Player said the purchase hung for several seconds with nothing
happening on screen, so they pressed buy again.

### PS-349 · 18 Mar · closed, won't fix

Store layout breaks on a narrow phone screen.

### PS-358 · Iron Siege · 22 Mar · open

Agent note: third player this quarter reporting an order that shows as failed
when the items did arrive. No pattern found. Suggest closing unless it comes up
again.

### PS-366 · Tide Farm · 29 Mar · open

"I bought the tractor and got nothing." Player is level 40 and the order shows
as delivered. The agent could not reproduce it.

---

Agents use the support console in the store for this. It shows what each game
reports back about a player. Nobody has checked it against what the store
believes happened.
